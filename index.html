Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

Sevora ("we", "our", "us") operates the Sevora mobile application. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Name and email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Location (city/neighborhood you enter, not GPS tracking)

When you book or provide services:

  • Booking details (date, time, service type, price)
  • Messages exchanged between customers and providers
  • Payment information (processed securely by Stripe — we never see or store your full card number)

Automatically collected:

  • Device token for push notifications (only if you grant permission)
  • Basic app usage data for crash reporting

How We Use Your Information

  • To connect customers with service providers
  • To process bookings and payments
  • To send booking confirmations, reminders, and messages
  • To display provider profiles to customers
  • To improve the app experience

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Ever.

Payment Processing

All payments are processed by Stripe. When you make a payment, your card details go directly to Stripe's secure servers. We only receive confirmation that payment succeeded or failed.

Data Sharing

We share information only as needed to provide the service:

  • Between customers and providers: your name, profile photo, and messages are visible to the other party in a booking
  • Stripe: payment processing
  • Apple Push Notification service: delivering notifications to your device

Data Retention

We retain your account data as long as your account is active. Booking records are kept for accounting and dispute resolution purposes. You can request deletion at any time.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Access and update your profile information in the app
  • Delete your account and all associated data (in Account settings)
  • Opt out of push notifications (in your device Settings)
  • Request a copy of your data by contacting us

Children's Privacy

Sevora is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll notify you of significant changes through the app or by email.
