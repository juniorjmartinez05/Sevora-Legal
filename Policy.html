Privacy Policy

Effective August 7, 2026 · Last updated August 14, 2026

Sevora is a booking marketplace that connects clients with independent service professionals — barbers, stylists, photographers, nail artists, and trainers — in Philadelphia. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Sevora is operated by Junior Martinez, an independent developer based in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. In this policy, "Sevora," "we," and "us" refer to that operator. "You" refers to anyone who uses the Sevora app, whether as a client or as a service professional.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password when you create an account. Passwords are stored only as salted cryptographic hashes; we never see or store your password in readable form.
  • Phone number — if you choose to add one to your profile.
  • Profile content — profile photos, and for service professionals, business descriptions, service listings, pricing, availability, and work-gallery photographs you upload.
  • Messages — the content of messages you send to other users through the app, including any images you attach.
  • Reviews and ratings you submit about a service professional.
  • Reports you file about other users, including the reason you select and any details you provide.

Information created when you use Sevora

  • Booking records — the services you book or provide, appointment dates and times, booking status, and price.
  • Transaction records — amounts charged, refund status, and payout status. See Section 4 regarding card details.
  • Search queries — the text you type into Sevora's search, such as "haircut around noon tomorrow." See Section 5.
  • Device tokens — an identifier issued by Apple that allows us to deliver push notifications to your device. You can revoke this at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Basic technical information — such as the app version in use, needed to operate and troubleshoot the service.

Information we do not collect

Sevora does not request or collect your device's location, contacts, calendar, microphone, health data, browsing history, or search history from outside the app. Distances shown next to a service professional are calculated from the neighborhood information they provide about their business, not from your device's location.

We may disclose information if we are legally required to do so, or where we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect the safety of a user or the public, or to investigate fraud or a violation of our terms.

If Sevora is ever acquired or merged, information may transfer as part of that transaction. We will notify you in the app before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

4. Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Sevora never receives or stores your full card number, security code, or bank account number. That information is transmitted directly from your device to Stripe. We receive only a payment confirmation, the last four digits of the card, and the transaction amount.

Service professionals who accept payments through Sevora must complete Stripe's onboarding, which requires identity and bank account information collected directly by Stripe under its own privacy policy. Stripe's handling of that information is described at stripe.com/privacy.

5. Search queries and automated processing

Sevora's search lets you describe what you need in plain language. To interpret those searches, the text you type is sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which returns a structured interpretation — the type of service and any date or time you mentioned.

Only the search text itself is sent. Your name, email, account identifier, and booking history are not included. Anthropic does not use data submitted through its API to train its models. If the interpretation service is unavailable, Sevora falls back to conventional keyword matching and your search still works.

6. Messaging, reporting, and safety

Messages between clients and service professionals are stored on our servers so they are available across sessions. They are not end-to-end encrypted, and they may be reviewed by us when a user files a report or where we have reason to believe our terms have been violated.

Every conversation includes options to report the conversation and to block the other person. Blocking removes the conversation from your view and prevents further contact. Reports are recorded with the reason you select and reviewed by us; we aim to review reports within 24 hours. We may remove content, suspend accounts, or ban users in response.

7. How long we keep information

  • Account and profile information — for as long as your account is active.
  • Booking and transaction records — retained after account deletion where required for tax, accounting, and fraud-prevention purposes, generally up to seven years.
  • Messages — retained while both accounts are active; deleted when either participant deletes their account, except where preserved as part of an open safety investigation.
  • Reports — retained for up to two years so that repeated patterns of abuse can be identified.
  • Search queries — not stored in association with your account.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from within the app: open Account, then Delete Account. Deletion removes your profile, photos, service listings, and messages, subject to the retention exceptions described above. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the app, email support@sevora.me from your registered address and we will process the deletion within 30 days.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction — you can view and edit your profile information at any time in the app.
  • Deletion — see Section 8.
  • Push notifications — can be disabled in iOS Settings under Notifications, or from within Sevora.
  • Photo library access — granted when you first upload a photo and revocable in iOS Settings.

California residents have the right under the CCPA to know what personal information we collect, to request its deletion, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. Sevora does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. To make a request, email us at the address below.

10. Children

Sevora is not directed to children and is not intended for use by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18, and users must be 18 or older to create an account or book services. If you believe someone under 18 has created an account or provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Data is transmitted between your device and our servers over TLS-encrypted connections and is encrypted at rest by our infrastructure provider. Access to production data is restricted, and database-level security rules limit each account to its own records. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected users without undue delay if a breach affecting personal information occurs.

12. Where information is processed

Sevora is operated from the United States, and information is stored and processed on servers located in the United States. Sevora is currently available only in the United States.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Sevora changes. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. If the changes are material, we will notify you in the app before they take effect. Continuing to use Sevora after a change means you accept the updated policy.
